1.Who We Are

Archyy (“Archyy,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a mobile application operated by [LEGAL ENTITY NAME], a company registered in Israel with its registered office at [REGISTERED ADDRESS] (the “Company”). The Company is the owner and manager of the personal-data database behind the app within the meaning of Israel's Privacy Protection Law, 5741-1981 (the “PPL”), and is the data controller of your personal data.

If you have any question about this policy or your data, contact us at hello@archyy.com.

2.Who This Policy Covers

Archyy accounts are held by adults — parents and legal guardians aged 18 or over. Account holders create a family profile and add their children to it. This policy therefore covers two kinds of people:

  • the parent or guardian who holds the account; and
  • the children whose details the parent or guardian chooses to add.

Children do not create or hold their own Archyy accounts. The policy also applies to the limited details you may provide about other adults — a second parent/guardian and an emergency contact. Please read the dedicated Children's Personal Data section below.

What we do not collect

Archyy is discovery-first. We do not process or store your payment-card details — when you choose to book or pay, the app hands you over to the third-party activity or booking provider, who handles the transaction on their own systems (see How We Share Information and the Terms of Service). We do not run third-party advertising trackers, and we do not sell your personal data.

4.How & Why We Use Your Information

We use the information above to:

  • create and manage your family account and profile;
  • match activities to your child — using grade, age (from date of birth), sex, school, location, and health-fund eligibility — so we can show programs that are relevant, age- and grade-appropriate, nearby, and that your child qualifies for;
  • show activities near your home and let you search by proximity, using your address and approximate coordinates;
  • save and show you your bookmarks, bookings, enrollments, and outing history;
  • hand you over to the relevant provider when you choose to book, passing along the details needed to start that booking (which may include the child's and the emergency contact's details, at your action);
  • keep the Service secure, prevent abuse, diagnose problems, and improve how the app works;
  • contact you about your account and important service or policy changes, and — only where you have agreed or the law allows — about relevant activities;
  • comply with our legal obligations.

Why we collect the sensitive fields

We collect a child's date of birth to confirm age-based eligibility precisely (many programs have strict age cut-offs) and to derive the grade/age used for matching. We collect health-fund / HMO membership because some activities, subsidies, and discounts in Israel are offered through, or in partnership with, the health funds, so membership determines what a child is eligible for. We collect sex because some programs are organized by sex. We ask for these details only to match your child to the right options — never for any unrelated purpose, and never to build advertising profiles.

5.Legal Bases for Processing

Under Israeli law, we rely primarily on your informed consent — given when you create your account and add your family's details — and on the necessity of processing to provide the Service you asked for. You may withdraw consent at any time (see Your Rights); doing so may mean we can no longer provide some or all of the Service.

If and to the extent the EU/EEA or UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / UK GDPR) applies to you, our legal bases are: performance of a contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b)); your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), including explicit consent for sensitive / special-category data such as health-fund membership and other health-related data (Art. 9(2)(a)); our legitimate interests in running, securing, and improving the Service (Art. 6(1)(f)); and compliance with legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(c)). Where a child's data is processed and a child's own online consent would otherwise be required, we rely on the consent and authorization of the holder of parental responsibility (Art. 8 GDPR).

6.Children's Personal Data

Protecting children's information is central to how Archyy works. Please read this section carefully.

Accounts are for adults

Only a parent or legal guardian aged 18 or over may create an Archyy account. Children do not create, hold, or sign in to their own accounts.

You provide the data & consent on the child's behalf

When you add a child to your family profile, you are providing that child's personal data and, as the person with parental responsibility, giving consent for us to process it for the purposes in this policy. You confirm you are entitled to do so.

What we collect about a child, and why

First and last name, school grade, sex, school (name and semel), date of birth, and health-fund / HMO membership — used solely to match the child to suitable, eligible, age- and grade-appropriate activities and outings, and to help you manage their bookings. We do not build advertising profiles of children or use their data for any purpose beyond running the Service.

Sensitive details get extra care

A child's date of birth and health-fund membership are treated as sensitive and protected with heightened security — encryption in transit and at rest, and strict access limits (see Security). They are collected for eligibility and matching, as explained in Section 4.

Retention

We keep a child's data only while the child is on your active family profile and for the limited period after removal or account closure described in Data Retention, after which it is deleted.

Your controls as a parent / guardian

At any time you can view, correct, or delete any child's details from within the app, remove a child from your profile, or ask us to do so at hello@archyy.com. Deleting a child removes their personal data from your profile, subject to the limited retention above.

EU / US note

Archyy is directed to families in Israel. If you use Archyy from the EU/EEA, Article 8 GDPR (and “GDPR-K” principles) applies to children's data; if from the United States, the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) may apply. Because every account is held, and all children's data is entered, by a parent or guardian, we rely on verifiable parental consent. If you believe a child's data has reached us without proper parental consent, contact hello@archyy.com and we will delete it.

7.How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only as described here.

  • Supabase (our data processor). We use Supabase to authenticate sign-ins and to host and store the app's data. Supabase processes this data on our behalf, under our instructions and appropriate data-protection terms.
  • Apple and Google (sign-in). If you sign in with Apple or Google, that provider verifies your identity and returns a confirmation and a basic account identifier to us. Your use of those sign-in services is also governed by Apple's and Google's own privacy policies.
  • Third-party activity & booking providers. Archyy links out to the activity provider, venue, municipality, or ticketing platform to complete a booking or payment. When you choose to proceed, you leave Archyy and the provider handles the booking and any payment on their own systems, as an independent data controller under their own terms and privacy policy. Any details you enter with them — including payment-card details — are collected by them, not by us. We are not a party to that transaction (see the Terms of Service).
  • Other service providers. We may use trusted vendors (for example cloud hosting, email delivery, and error diagnostics) that process limited data on our behalf under confidentiality and data-protection obligations.
  • Legal & safety. We may disclose information where required by law, legal process, or a competent authority, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users, the public, or the Company.
  • Business transfer. If the Company is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, personal data may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this policy.

Other adults' details. The second-guardian and emergency-contact details you provide are personal data about other people. By entering them, you confirm you are entitled to share them and that you will, where appropriate, let those people know their details are held in Archyy and may be passed to a provider at the time of a booking.

8.Data Retention

We keep personal data only as long as we need it for the purposes in this policy:

  • Account, family-profile, and children's data — for as long as your account is active.
  • After you delete a child, remove them from your profile, or close your account — we delete the associated personal data within a reasonable period (we aim for within 90 days), except where we must keep certain records longer to meet a legal obligation, resolve disputes, or enforce our agreements. Residual copies in secure backups are overwritten on our normal backup cycle.
  • Activity and booking history — kept while your account is active so you can see it, and removed with your account.

When data is no longer needed, we delete it or irreversibly anonymize it.

9.How We Protect Your Information

We take appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect personal data, consistent with the security-level obligations of the PPL's data-security regulations and good industry practice. These include encryption of data in transit and at rest, access controls that limit who can see personal data (with tighter limits on sensitive fields such as date of birth and health-fund membership), authentication through trusted providers, and ongoing monitoring. No system is perfectly secure, but we work hard to protect your family's information and to meet our breach-notification obligations if something goes wrong.

10.Your Rights

You have rights over your personal data. Under Israel's PPL, you may review the data we hold about you and your family and ask us to correct, complete, or delete data that is inaccurate, incomplete, or no longer needed. If the GDPR applies to you, you also have rights of access, rectification, erasure (“right to be forgotten”), restriction, objection, and data portability, and the right not to be subject to solely automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects.

You can exercise most of these rights directly in the app — view and edit your profile and your children's details, or delete your account. For anything else, email hello@archyy.com and we will respond within the timeframes the law requires. You may withdraw consent at any time; this will not affect processing already carried out.

If you believe we have mishandled your data, you can complain to the Israeli Privacy Protection Authority (PPA). EU/EEA users may also complain to their local data-protection supervisory authority.

11.International Data Transfers

We are based in Israel and direct Archyy to families in Israel. Some of our providers (including Supabase and the sign-in providers) may store or process data outside Israel, including in the EU/EEA and the United States. When data is transferred abroad, we do so in accordance with the PPL's transfer rules (including the Privacy Protection Regulations (Transfer of Data to Databases Abroad), 2001) and, where the GDPR applies, on the basis of an adequacy decision (Israel benefits from an EU adequacy recognition) or appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

12.Cookies & Analytics

Archyy is a native mobile app, not a website, so it does not use browser cookies for advertising. We do not use third-party advertising networks, cross-app tracking, or ad-profiling SDKs, and we do not sell data. We use only the first-party technical identifiers needed to sign you in and keep your session secure (via Supabase), and we may use minimal, privacy-respecting diagnostics to find and fix crashes and errors. If we ever introduce analytics or any tracking that requires it, we will update this policy and obtain your consent where the law requires.
